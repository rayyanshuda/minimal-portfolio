Hook me to a polygraph
And ask me if I love you
I’ll say no
But then we’ll watch the needle sputter
Exactly how you laugh
How you felt my heartbeat when you laid on my chest
How your lips pulsed when its crevices fell into mine
We’ll watch it draw the shape of mountains
That we promised each other we’d go see together
And then die down like waves
To the ocean we made plans to go to
