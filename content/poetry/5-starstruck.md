Bright eyes
Like starlight
That stops time
And silences the mind

I tried
To look away
But something inside
Pulled me like a tide

Paralyzed
By the sight of her
A quiet riot
Behind my ribs

She smiled
And the whole sky
Rearranged itself
Into something divine

I realized
Right then
That I had never truly
Seen light

Until she
Turned those eyes
And looked
Inside mine
