id: guts
title: Guts
caption: 11.08.2026 - a boxer who lost his father before the fight cant seem to fight during a match. his father was his biggest supporter.
order: 8
---

today i brought my own guts
but i couldn’t bring my strength
because you held that
and now that you’re gone
i cant seem to lift it
why is it so heavy?

the floor is cold
there are no cheers
i have a crowd behind me
but not you

the tears are streaming from my eyes
perhaps you held onto them too
and now that you’re gone
i cant seem to hold back

the lights are shining
like you did in my corner
drowning everything else out

i brought my guts
i brought my heart
i brought my feet in front of the lights
so why is it *still* so dark?
