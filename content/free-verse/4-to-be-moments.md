those moments just so happened to be memories. i remember in great detail the way you looked at me. with strong conviction in your eyes that i was the only one you saw this way. was i a fool to believe it? hope is a dangerous thing they say. i ripped my heart from my chest, tearing through bone and muscle until my ribs felt like an empty, ruined cage. leaving my nerves open-ended, i laid the pulsing weight of it into your hands. i became frigid. detached from everyone but you. i was in a cave with you as my beacon. i was a moth attracted to your light. i followed without seeing. it was hard to see but when i saw. you were not looking at me anymore. what i thought we had was a glimpse of what i had been holding onto. was i a fool to believe you were holding on as well?
